Our Mission

We Bring Pakistan to the World — and the World to Pakistan

Founded in 2012 by a group of passionate mountain enthusiasts, Roshan Travels started with a single goal: to share the incredible beauty of Pakistan with anyone who dared to look. What began as a small Islamabad-based startup has grown into Pakistan's most loved travel company with over 5,000 satisfied clients across the globe.

We believe Pakistan is one of the world's most underrated travel destinations — and we're on a mission to change that. From the icy peaks of K2 to the warm sands of Gwadar, every inch of this land has a story worth telling. We tell those stories through unforgettable journeys.

Why Us

What Makes Us Different

We're not just a travel agency — we're your adventure partner, your safety net, and your local guide all in one.

Born in the Mountains

Our founders are mountain enthusiasts who've personally trekked every major route in Pakistan. We know these lands like our own backyard.

PTDC Certified

We are officially registered with Pakistan Tourism Development Corporation (PTDC) and follow all industry standards and regulations.

Transparent PKR Pricing

All prices are in Pakistani Rupees. No foreign currency confusion, no hidden charges, no surprise fees. What you see is what you pay.

Expert Local Guides

All our guides are certified, first-aid trained, and have deep local knowledge. Your safety on every trail is our topmost priority.

Responsible Tourism

We practice and promote eco-friendly travel. We minimize waste, support local communities, and give back to the environments we visit.

Personal Touch

We're not a faceless corporation. When you call us, a real human answers — someone who loves travel as much as you do.

Our Journey

From a Small Idea to a Big Adventure

Every great journey begins with a single step. Ours began with a campfire in Fairy Meadows and a dream to share Pakistan with the world.

2012
Founding Year

Roshan Travels is Born

Three friends with a shared passion for mountains launch a small travel agency from Islamabad with just 2 staff members.

2015
Growth Phase

First 500 Clients & PTDC Certification

We crossed 500 happy travelers and received our official PTDC certification — a major milestone in building trust.

2018
Expansion

Expanded to Southern Pakistan

Launched beach and coastal tours to Gwadar, Ormara, and the Makran Coastline — bringing new regions to light.

2021
Digital Leap

Online Booking Platform Launched

Introduced our online booking system, making it easier than ever for travelers across Pakistan to plan their dream trips.

2025
Today

5,000+ Travelers & Growing

With a team of 35+ professionals, 50+ destinations, and 5,000+ happy travelers — our journey continues stronger than ever.
