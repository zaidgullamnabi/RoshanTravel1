FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

How do I book a tour with Roshan Travels?
You can book online through our website (click "Book Now" on any package), via WhatsApp, by phone, or by visiting our office. We'll confirm your booking within 2 hours and guide you through the payment process.
What is your cancellation/refund policy?
• 7+ days before departure: 90% refund
• 3–6 days before: 50% refund
• Less than 3 days: No refund (except force majeure situations)
• We also offer free rescheduling up to 48 hours before departure subject to availability.
Can you create a custom package for our group?
Absolutely! We specialize in custom tours. Fill out the contact form with your requirements (dates, group size, budget, destinations, accommodation preferences) and our team will create a personalized itinerary and quote within 24 hours — completely free.
Are your packages suitable for families with children?
Yes! Many of our packages are family-friendly. Swat Valley, Murree, Lahore Heritage, and Hunza Valley tours are particularly popular with families. We adjust itineraries for children's comfort and can arrange special activities. Mention family requirements when booking.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept: Bank Transfer (all major Pakistani banks), EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Cash (at our office), and Installment Plans for packages above PKR 50,000. A 30% deposit confirms your booking with the balance due 3 days before departure.
Is travel insurance included in the packages?
Travel insurance is NOT included by default but is strongly recommended, especially for adventure/trekking tours. We can arrange travel insurance for you at an additional cost. Please ask our team for options when booking.
Do you arrange tours for international tourists?
Yes! We welcome international travelers and can help with NOC (No Objection Certificate) arrangements for restricted areas like Khunjerab Pass and some trekking routes. We also provide airport pickup, visa assistance guidance, and translation services.
